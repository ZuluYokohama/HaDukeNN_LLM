Primary comparisons

  • hnt vs equal-size control. G1: hnt64k against sw64k, the equal-size superword-BPE control, at one vocabulary size.
  • best arm vs struct32k / GPT-2. G1 holds hnt64k to struct32k on test BPB and train FLOPs per byte. H1 keeps GPT-2 BPE on the same train bytes. G4 holds the full arm to the tuned baseline at matched all-in FLOPs.
  • pe_heat vs baseline. P2 holds the heat and heat+RoPE positional encodings to the tuned modern baseline.

Highlighting a gate does not mean the campaign passed it. G0 is partial because V=65,536 is over 64,000. G1 has failed. G1b, the structured hnt40k follow-up, has also failed on BPB; it is not a separate highlighted node. Later gates are not run.
