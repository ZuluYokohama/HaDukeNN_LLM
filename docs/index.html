Primary comparisons

These are the comparisons the control graph is built to score. The campaign has not passed them. G0 is partial, G1 has failed, and G1b has failed. The diagram is on the control graph page. Highlighted gates are G1, H1, G4, and P2.

  • hnt vs equal-size control. G1 compares hnt64k with sw64k, the equal-size superword-BPE control, at the same vocabulary size.
  • best arm vs struct32k / GPT-2. G1 compares hnt64k with struct32k on test BPB and train FLOPs per byte. H1 keeps GPT-2 BPE on the same train bytes. G4 compares the full arm with the tuned baseline at matched all-in FLOPs.
  • pe_heat vs baseline. P2 scores the heat and heat+RoPE positional encodings against the tuned modern baseline.
