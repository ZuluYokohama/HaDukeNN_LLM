G1c pass is compute-matched, not a per-byte modeling win. g1d beats hnt at equal data. g1g beats g1d only at equal analytic FLOPs, because its factored head counts about 2.22× fewer FLOPs per token and so sees about 2.2× the data. Task conditions

Situation
The public tree is the harness, synthetic self-checks, and recorded metadata. It is not a trained model and it does not hold a corpus.
Mission
Keep every later claim tied to hashes, a ledger, and the gates in GATES.md.
Constraints

Standard

That margin comes from seeing about 2.2× the data, and it reverses at equal data (G1c vs g1i, −0.058) and on wall clock (about 3× slower). Wall-clock and 2× data-ladder controls are pending. g1d measured Data-matched struct32k at 348.3M bytes, the same train bytes hnt40k-struct-v2 saw. Test bits per byte 1.194155 ± 0.000824 at 1.0756e16 train FLOPs. That beats hnt by 0.075 at equal data.
g1g measured g1g, hnt40k-struct-v2 FLOPs-matched to g1d at 1.0756e16 train FLOPs, scored 1.174315 ± 0.000564 (seeds 1.174805, 1.173698, 1.174444). That is 0.019839 below g1d (Welch p 1.3e-5), That is a win at equal analytic train FLOPs only (the 6N + attention + head count). The structured model swaps the flat 40k output softmax for a factored cluster-then-unit head (200 clusters × 200 units). At d=384 the flat head is about 56.6% of plain-model FLOPs per token, so struct-v2 counts about 2.22× fewer FLOPs per token. At equal FLOPs, g1g therefore saw 891.7M train bytes (about 1.65 epochs, against g1d's 0.64), and it took about 1,488 s per seed against about 501–505 s for g1d and g1h, so it is about 3× slower on wall clock.
g1h measured g1h, plain hnt40k with the structured path off (struct none, mean compose, low-rank residual, no spectral init) at the same 1.0756e16 train FLOPs, scored 1.197637 ± 0.000517 (seeds 1.198146, 1.197111, 1.197653). That is 0.023321 above g1g (p 8.4e-7) and 0.003482 above g1d (p 0.006): at equal analytic FLOPs and the same tokenizer, the structured run scores lower, but it also saw about 2.2× the data. At equal data the order reverses. G1c, the same hnt40k-struct-v2 model at 348.3M train bytes, is 1.269376 ± 0.004417. That is 0.0579 worse than plain hnt40k at the same bytes (g1i, Welch p 8.8e-5) and 0.0752 worse than struct32k (g1d, p 8.2e-4). The mechanism resembles a class-factored softmax (Goodman 2001, arXiv:cs/0108006). This is not evidence that the structure layer models text better. Wall-clock-matched and 2× data-ladder controls are pending.
g1i measured g1i, the same plain hnt40k at g1d's 348.35M train bytes (9.350e15 train FLOPs), scored 1.211442 ± 0.003395 (seeds 1.210431, 1.215228, 1.208666). That is 0.017287 above g1d (p 0.0094), so at equal bytes plain hnt40k loses to struct32k. g1i and g1d use different tokenizers, so this is a tokenizer contrast, not a structure ablation.
A3 running A3, the random-cluster control (hnt40k with random clusters, FLOPs-matched), finished as g1j: 1.176685 ± 0.002032 (n=3). That is 0.002369 above g1g (Welch two-sided p 0.17). It is not a clean contrast, because spectral init was on in g1g and off in g1j. Untested.
G2–G6 not run Spectral, kernel, headline, scaling, and motif gates are not claimed.

Primary comparisons

These are the comparisons the control graph is built to score. G0 is partial. G1 has failed. G1b has failed. G1c passes on a compute-matched budget and does not pass as a per-byte model: g1d's struct32k is 0.075 bits per byte lower at the same 348.3M bytes. g1g beats g1d by 0.019839 at equal train FLOPs. The diagram is on the control graph page. Highlighted gates are G1, H1, G4, and P2.

  • hnt vs equal-size control. G1 compares hnt64k with sw64k, the equal-size superword-BPE control, at the same vocabulary size.
  • best arm vs struct32k / GPT-2. G1 compares hnt64k with struct32k on test BPB and train FLOPs per byte. H1 keeps GPT-2 BPE on the same train bytes. G4 compares the full arm with the tuned baseline at matched all-in FLOPs.
  • pe_heat vs baseline. P2 scores the heat and heat+RoPE positional encodings against the tuned modern baseline. It has not been run: every recorded run applied RoPE.

Since g1i

These folders are on main under iterations/.

  • t37-grid. T-37 tokenizer scaling grid on FineWeb-Edu, flat heads, fwedu-hnt40k against fwedu-struct32k, 2 sizes × 4 byte budgets, n=3. struct32k is better in all 8 contrasts, and Holm (α 0.01) keeps 5 of 8. The edge shrinks with data (log2 k slope −0.232 at S, −0.137 at M) and is larger at M than at S (+0.125, 95% CI +0.080 to +0.173). These are tokenizer effects, not structured-head effects.
  • t38-design. Frozen T-38 pre-registration (SHA-256 524fca09…, path-redacted public copy) and a paired-seed addendum (reported secondary only). Plain against the structure component on the same 32k BPE tokenizer. No result yet.
  • hg-e1-pack-random. E1 packing control, random article order: E1 held-out-article BPB 1.093936 ± 0.003312 (n=3). It has no pass/fail of its own.
  • t39-frontier-blueprint. Next-level verification blueprint: 256 × 128 Tensor-Core-aligned spectral cluster geometry, noise-buffered Stage-3 L-fit proposal, verified train-only hg-v3 metadata gate, and T-39 two-part large-vocabulary plus sheaf KV-prefix pre-registration template.
  • LEDGER_since_g1i.csv. Every iteration recorded after g1i, numbers only, with no verdict labels.
