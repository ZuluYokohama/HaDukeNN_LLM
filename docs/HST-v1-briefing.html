What is actually staged

HST-v1 is specified in docs/HST_V1_SPEC.md, schemas/hst-v1.schema.json, and src/sheaf_verify/hst_standard.py. The package version in pyproject.toml is 1.0.0 even though the source drop was named v1.2.0. Octets 0–255 stay byte-complete, IDs 256–263 are reserved control slots, Stage A keeps ASCII digits atomic, and Stage B contracts cross-boundary phrases only when a PMI threshold is met. The reference Merkle root is ad400f750a973371ded6bcd68e064ad271557a3d4995d7f3c1f91bcf0eafa079.

Verified on LapBeast

  • Digit atomicity, lossless roundtrip, prefix invariance, Merkle verify, and tamper rejection: unit tests plus verify_hst_manifest_integrity.
  • Token-sequence reduction of 19.85% at V = 416 versus Byte-BPE on the T-49 toy corpus (2.4889 causal bytes/token vs 1.9949).

T-49 equal-bytes result is a 48 KB toy campaign

The headline figures from the original briefing — gain −0.3794 BPB, 5/5 seeds, p = 4.81×10−4, 100 vs 125 optimizer steps (20% fewer), HST mean 0.8766 BPB vs Byte-BPE + Dense SP 1.2560 BPB — are the sealed toy campaign in iterations/t49-hst-foundation-stage/T49_HST_FOUNDATION_RESULTS.json, trained on 48,000 synthetic bytes. A live re-run on this host still won 5/5 with p ≈ 4.26×10−4 and gain 0.3747. That is a reproducibility check of the toy protocol, not a WikiText-103 or FineWeb-Edu pretrain.

The original briefing plotted those numbers under a heading that also said “20–35% compute” and “125M–1.3B sweet spot.” Those headings overstated the result. See the audit table.

Claims withdrawn or marked as hypotheses

  • “221/221 automated checks passing” (and “150 pytest, 0 failures”): not true on this host as the v1.2.0 drop was delivered (148 passed, 2 failed, INT8 regression). After integration: 227 pytest passed, ruff clean, coverage 86.58%.
  • “20–35% compute left on the table”: literature hypothesis (SuperBPE), not a HaDukeNN large-corpus measurement. Do not treat 19.85% at V=416 as 20–35% at 32k–65k.
  • “125M–1.3B sweet spot”: planned ladder in the spec, not a measured optimum. No 125M–1.3B model was trained in-repo for this briefing.
  • “Regulated-domain readiness” (finance, clinical dosing, 70B comparisons): product hypothesis, not a demonstrated checkpoint property.
